\subsection{Full-battery estimates and composition}
\label{sec:composition}

Accuracy carries little seed signal at the benchmark level, where reliability averages 0.324 against 0.652 on margins, and Table~\ref{tab:original3} and Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary} give the per-benchmark diagnostics, none of which enter the headline ratio because it uses covariance products directly.

The factor belongs to this battery, since margin correlations track scoring format (cloze for HellaSwag, PIQA and WinoGrande, yes/no for BoolQ, and multiple choice for the other six), averaging 0.624 within formats and 0.007 across them, excluding WinoGrande's undefined correlations (Figure~\ref{fig:covariance} in Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary}), and BoolQ, the only benchmark in its format, carries 68\% of the margin covariance trace and 84\% of the accuracy trace. Removing it raises margin inflation to 1.786 \ci{1.689}{1.877}, and accuracy inflation to 1.558 \ci{1.412}{1.687}, while removing CommonsenseQA instead gives inflation estimates of 1.096 and 1.014. The removal estimates imply off-diagonal terms involving BoolQ summing to about $-0.15$ of the margin trace and $-0.06$ of the accuracy trace. Its removal lowers the denominator of Equation~\ref{eq:lambda} more than the numerator, which raises the factor while the margin aggregate standard deviation falls from 0.00572 to 0.00516, because the factor compares each battery with independence at its own marginal variances. Lowering BoolQ's weight from a tenth to 0.0867 already stops the accuracy interval including one (Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary}).

Shared passages don't account for BoolQ's trace share, although that check can't reach other shared item effects. We recovered the 2,938 passages behind BoolQ's 3,270 questions from the release's request files, and assigning each passage whole to one half changes the two estimates by $-0.0002$ and $-0.0001$ on average across fifty re-splits. The BoolQ diagonal moves by 0.03\% and 0.15\% (Appendix~\ref{app:derivations}). A permutation that reshuffles each run's item deviations inside each of the 66 item groups, MMLU's 57 subjects and the other nine benchmarks, leaves all trait scores fixed and breaks the tie between particular items and runs. Across 2,000 such permutations the permuted estimates sit about 0.001 below the observed margin value and 0.002 below accuracy, so item-level sharing accounts for little of either, although an effect carried by every item of a benchmark survives this permutation untouched.

Benchmark removal moves our estimates more than recipe or size removal. Recipe removal moves margin inflation between 1.207 and 1.256, and all 300 recipe-pair deletions keep the interval above one, with estimates from 1.177 to 1.263 and no lower limit below 1.037 (Appendix~\ref{app:supplementary}). Size removal moves it between 1.178 and 1.289, where the deletion of 530M gives \ci{0.993}{1.338} and stops excluding one. Figure~\ref{fig:composition} shows every single-benchmark removal with its interval on both scales, and Appendix~\ref{app:composition} separates benchmark count, identity, and weights. Our design treats this battery as fixed, and that spread isn't a distribution over benchmark collections.

\subsection{A pre-specified test on held-out tasks}
\label{sec:heldout}
The pre-specified rule fails on held-out tasks. Held-out margin inflation on the 225 runs at 530M, 750M, and 1B is 1.217 \ci{0.872}{1.498}, while the original ten on the same runs give 1.240 \ci{1.054}{1.397} and pass it, a comparison we specified before any held-out score existed. The two point estimates differ by about 0.02, the held-out interval is 0.626 wide against 0.343 for the original, and our rule reads the lower limit.

The protocol named SciQ, MedMCQA, and two AGIEval \citep{zhong2024agieval} reasoning tasks, with a pre-specified rule replacing any task whose 1B pilot accuracy fell below chance plus 0.02, a bar both AGIEval tasks narrowly missed (Appendix~\ref{app:heldout}). The battery became SciQ, MedMCQA, and multiple-choice versions of DROP and CoQA, with 1,000 items in each of the four tasks and items sharing a passage or story kept in the same half. Our GPU budget couldn't cover all 375 released checkpoints, and our protocol's scope rule limited the test to those runs at the steps of the primary analysis. The test passes only if the lower 95\% limit exceeds one, and Table~\ref{tab:heldout} reports the test at its pre-specified scope. Every partial scope we read before the full one also failed the rule, with held-out margin estimates between 1.152 and 1.203, and Appendix~\ref{app:heldout} lists all five with a power simulation of the rule at each scope.

As an exploratory result, the held-out accuracy interval excludes one while the margin interval doesn't, so which scale's interval excludes one depends on the battery. Accuracy inflation is 1.220 \ci{1.066}{1.359}, above the original ten's 1.097 at the same scope. Removing CoQA lowers held-out margin inflation to 1.090, while removing any other task leaves it between 1.120 and 1.213, and only the removal of DROP, at 1.213 \ci{1.035}{1.377}, excludes one, so the held-out estimate depends on battery composition as the original does (Appendix~\ref{app:heldout}).
