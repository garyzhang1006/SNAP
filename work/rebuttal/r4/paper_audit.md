# Round 4 paper audit (gate mode, reviewer pass)

Target: `deliverables/main.tex` (256 lines), with `appendix_a.tex` and `appendices_bcd.tex` read for cross-checks. Execution: sequential single-agent, read-only, no compute. Line numbers refer to `main.tex` unless an appendix file is named.

## Gate decision

**PASS (no submission blockers), with six score-lowering findings.**

The script gate (`audit.py --mode gate`) returned FAIL, and every blocking item comes from the known false positives. The undefined `\ref`s point to appendix labels the script can't see (`app:*`, `tab:notation`, `tab:coverage`, `tab:prediction`, `tab:decision`, `fig:covariance`, and `S-#1` from the `xr` macro). The anonymity flag fires on the intended `Anonymous authors` block. The acronym flag (SNAP, DROP, ARC, OLMES, STATE) is noise, apart from SNAP in the abstract (PA14). Script advisories worth keeping are the unreferenced `eq:scores`, `eq:model` and `eq:effective` labels and the missing `~` before `\citet` in Related work. Neither can lower a score.

The EIC screen finds no desk-reject issue, since the paper is in scope, anonymous, fits nine pages with one outcome variant selected, and states its limits. The findings below are reviewer judgment ([LLM]). The numbers I recomputed all check out: K_eff for each Table 1 row, r̄_E = 0.038 at 1.157, the BoolQ off-diagonal sums of -0.15 and -0.06, σ_agg falling from 0.00572 to 0.00516, and the held-out interval widths of 0.626 and 0.343. I found no arithmetic contradiction in the main text.
