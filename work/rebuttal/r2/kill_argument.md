## 1. Attack memo (codex thread 1, verbatim)

Paper establishes sensitivity of one battery under assumptions it cannot verify, while its own stated alternative can reproduce headline.

## 3. Independent ruling on the adjudication

P1, P2 and P3 describe one objection and I rule on them together as KA1. Codex marked P2 still_unresolved, which ignores main.tex:75, where cross-format pairs average 0.007 with a diagnostic of 0.921 and the paper states they constrain a uniform component of that size. A scalar item effect shared by all ten benchmarks would raise every cross-format covariance, so the uniform form of the alternative is already partly answered and only a component that follows scoring format survives. The paper weakens its own defence in two ways, since no appendix shows the arithmetic behind "constrain a uniform component of that size" (the 0.921 diagnostic appears at appendices_bcd.tex:128 without that calculation), and the text never names a concrete mechanism. A prompt-format artefact that shifts a run's scores on every item of a template, the example given at main.tex:75, is a run-by-format effect that would reappear on any redrawn bank in that format, which makes it part of the fixed-battery run covariance a practitioner wants counted. The mechanism that would actually contaminate the estimand is something tied to the specific item set and shared by both halves, most plausibly each benchmark's fixed few-shot exemplar prefix (main.tex:135 filters bank two against "its own few-shot exemplars", so the prompts are few-shot). Whether bank two shares bank one's exemplars is not stated, and if it does the R2 cross-bank rule cannot separate that component either (unverified). My verdict for KA1 is partially_answered at critical, because the paper over-concedes a vague alternative without showing the calculation that already rules out most of it.

P4 stands as still_unresolved, and I lower it to major because the paper states it openly (main.tex:241) and the pending R2 check gives an analogous test in a second family.

P5 is conceded rather than answered, and I combine it with the underpowered R1 design into KA3.

P6 stands as partially_answered at major, since main.tex:245 already says the ratio "measures this battery", which a reader only reaches on page 9.

The attack missed the argument I consider more damaging on significance (KA2), where the paper's own evidence says the covariance barely changes real decisions and that its fitted ratio predicts worse than independence.

Arithmetic I checked by hand and found consistent: K_eff 6.46, 6.34, 8.60 and 8.51 from Table 1, the K_eff interval 5.6 to 7.7, r_E of 0.038 and K_eff of 7.47 at the accuracy upper limit, the effective coefficient 0.0608, the BoolQ-removal fall of sigma_agg from 0.00572 to 0.00516, and the BoolQ off-diagonal sum of about -0.15 of the margin trace. The recipe-pair deletion claim at main.tex:47 is supported only in supplement_extended_body.tex:627 and not in the submitted appendices. Page length was not checked beyond the note that the default build prints every outcome variant (42 pages total).
