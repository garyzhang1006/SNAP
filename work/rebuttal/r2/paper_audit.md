## Gate decision

**PASS, with advisory findings.** No submission blocker survives verification.

- The script printed FAIL on 17 "undefined references" (`app:derivations`, `tab:notation`, `S-#1`, ...). All of them are false positives, because the checker reads only `main.tex`, and every label exists in `appendix_a.tex` or `appendices_bcd.tex`, while `S-#1` is the `\suptab`/`\supfig` macro body resolved through `xr`. `deliverables/main.log` (built 00:50, same timestamp as `main.tex`) contains zero `undefined` warnings and no LaTeX warnings. Every `\cite` key resolves to a `\bibitem` in `references.tex`.
- The script's anonymity FAIL is also a false positive, because the author block reads `Anonymous authors` and `pdfauthor={}`.
- `maintext:end` sits on page 10 in the default build, which prints every outcome variant. The single-outcome build ending on page 9 was not rebuilt here because this pass is read-only.
- EIC screen: Conditional Pass (pitch 6, venue fit 8, fatal flaws 8, presentation 6, weighted 7.0). The research question is clear by the end of intro paragraph 2, but the abstract and the first intro paragraph each concede a limitation before the contribution lands.
- The pending-outcome scaffolding is intentional. PA3 flags one branch that has no text yet.
