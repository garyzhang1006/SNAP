## Gate decision: FAIL

The one real blocker is length. `main.aux` places `maintext:end` on page 11 (`\newlabel{maintext:end}{{6}{11}...}`), and ICLR 2027 caps the main text at 9 pages, which makes this a desk-reject risk before any reviewer reads it. With one outcome selected the text still ends near the top of page 11, so at about 54 lines per page the paper has to lose at least 60 lines. The cut plan below finds 74 lines in its first seven cuts and 38 more in reserve.

The script gate also printed FAIL, but for reasons that don't hold up. I checked each one:

- `[Script]` The "Undefined reference" blockers (`app:design`, `app:supplementary`, `app:derivations`, `tab:notation`, `S-#1`, and others) are false positives. `check_references.py` reads `main.tex` alone, while the labels sit in the `\input` appendix files (`appendices_bcd.tex:2`, `:79`, `appendix_a.tex:2`, `:13`) or come through `xr`. `main.log` has 0 "undefined" warnings.
- `[Script]` The anonymity FAIL is a false positive, because the author block is `\author{Anonymous authors}` and `pdfauthor={}`.
- `[Script]` The acronym FAIL flags benchmark names (PIQA, MMLU) and "RTX". Nothing needs fixing.
- `[Script]` The long-paragraph warnings (G2) at lines 133, 207, 213 and 226 are advisory. They point at the same paragraphs as the cut plan.
- `[Script]` Advisory items: the unnumbered-label equation at line 64 (L4) and the unused labels `eq:effective` and `eq:information` (L5). The L1 `~\cite` warnings are wrong for `\citet` used as a sentence subject.
- The red `\pending{}` placeholders and the labelled outcome variants in Section 4.3 are intentional and are left out of every finding.

Counts: 21 findings, of which 5 are major, 10 moderate and 6 minor, and 14 are likely to lower the score.
