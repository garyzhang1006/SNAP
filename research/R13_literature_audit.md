None of the seven closest studies estimates the covariance of training-seed noise between benchmarks, and none reports an effective benchmark count for a fixed battery under seed noise. Bouthillier's 1 + (k - 1) rho is over repeated trainings of one pipeline on one task, so the algebra is shared and the estimand is not. The manuscript's route is B in the plan's terms: a measurement paper on a reproducible cross-benchmark covariance, with the cross-half estimator as an established split-half construction applied to a new pairing (runs by item halves), and the novelty statement in the manuscript should say exactly that.
