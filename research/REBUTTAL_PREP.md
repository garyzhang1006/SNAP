## What would close the items that are not answered

Row 1 would move only with more held-out tasks scored on the same 225 runs, since the fail is a width result, and that needs compute. Row 2 would close with a second replicate population whose battery does not contain a single benchmark carrying 84 percent of the accuracy trace, which means scoring new runs and therefore needs compute. Row 3 would close with a decision setting that carries a nominal level, or failing that with an abstract that leads on the nominal-level result rather than on the recipe calls, and the reframe needs no compute. Row 4 would close only with an independent item pool per benchmark scored on the same checkpoints, since the appendix already shows the regression diagnostic has no power, and that needs compute. Row 5 would close with at least one benchmark scored in two formats on the same runs, which needs compute. Row 6 would close with a 750M rescore at steps matched across all three runs near their released final steps, which needs compute. Row 7 would close only with a third-party timestamp on the plan file, or by dropping its thresholds entirely, and neither needs compute. Row 8 would close with a release whose seed labels are crossed against recipes rather than shared inside a size band, which is outside our control and not a compute question.
